The ocean carries a person kilometres in a few hours, and nobody knows exactly where. Our drift model turns one splash into 10,000 places they could be. You get one helicopter, a 185 m strip of sea it can see, and 45 minutes. Then the Coast Guard's pattern and the AI search the same sea, and we reveal where the person really was.

Beat both the Coast Guard and the AI and you win a prize.
